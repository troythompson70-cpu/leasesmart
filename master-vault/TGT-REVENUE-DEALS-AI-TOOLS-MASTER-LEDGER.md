## Operating rules

1. Do **not** claim a vendor is approved unless the vendor actually approved TGT.
2. Do **not** infer commercial-use, client-use, retention, licensing, or reseller rights.
3. Separate **internal-use/NFR**, **trial**, **client-use**, **reseller**, and **commercial** rights.
4. Mark contracts, payment commitments, exclusivity, licensing agreements, likeness/voice/avatar rights, paid-ad rights, sublicensing, or model-training rights as **OWNER APPROVAL REQUIRED**.
5. Use **Troy Thompson, Founder & CEO, TGT Technologies Inc.** for business/legal correspondence.
6. Use **Tee Gates** only for creator/media/entertainment context.
7. Record the source, date verified, exact offer, limitations, next action, and current status.
8. Chat history is **not** the source of truth when it conflicts with this Microsoft 365 record (or this repo mirror of it).
