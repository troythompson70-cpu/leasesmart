# TGT Technologies Inc. — Founder Identity Standard

**Effective:** 2026-09-05  
**Status:** FINAL (PM directive)  
**Owner approval authority:** Tee Gates  

## Standard identity

| Context | Name to use |
|---|---|
| Brand / founder / media / operational / documentation | **Tee Gates** |
| Legal identity only where specifically required | **Troy Thompson** |

**Do not use:** `T. Gates`

## Use Tee Gates for

- Marketing, social media, sponsorships, creator campaigns, brand outreach
- Media kits, AI Gates campaigns, public-facing websites
- Product reviews, unboxings, commercial campaign materials
- Dashboards, handoffs, business-development documents
- Internal operating documents, Master Vault, project documentation
- Creator marketplace profiles, partnership / sales / presentation materials
- AI-avatar and likeness-licensing materials (naming — not legal acceptance)

## Use Troy Thompson only when specifically required for

- Contracts and legal agreements
- Tax documentation, banking, payment verification
- KYC, identity verification, platform verification
- Rights ownership, regulatory records, corporate legal records

## Fail-closed rule

If context is unclear whether brand or legal identity is required:

1. Do **not** change a legally required name.
2. Flag the document for review.
3. Prefer **Tee Gates** only when the document is clearly brand / operational and does **not** require legal identity.

## Related governance

- Microsoft 365 remains the permanent operational/document source of truth.
- Claude reviews licensing-risk / legal-term architecture.
- Tee Gates retains sole final owner approval for TGT operational and commercial decisions.
- No automation may bypass owner approval for likeness, voice, AI-avatar licensing, exclusivity, payments, or rights transfers.
